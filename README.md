# Marketing-assignment
E-commerce Sales & US Holiday Trends Analysis
📌 Overview
This project explores the impact of key US holidays on e-commerce transaction behavior and revenue. By merging transaction data with a dataset of US holiday dates, this analysis identifies peak revenue days, high-performing geographic markets, and key customer purchasing patterns to help marketing and inventory teams make data-driven promotional decisions.

🎯 Objectives
Clean and prepare e-commerce sales data, filtering out returns/cancellations.

Merge sales records with official US holiday dates to analyze holiday-specific purchasing patterns.

Engineer metrics such as total Revenue (Quantity × UnitPrice).

Identify top revenue-generating holidays, days of the week, and top geographical markets.

Deliver actionable business recommendations for campaign planning and stock management.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries
Language: Python 3.x

Data Manipulation: pandas, numpy

Data Visualization: plotnine (ggplot2 implementation for Python), matplotlib, seaborn

Environment: Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

📊 Key Findings
Top Performing Holiday:

Thanksgiving Eve generated the highest overall holiday revenue, totaling $13,587.13 across 665 orders (representing ~18.8% of total holiday revenue analyzed).

Columbus Day and Veterans Day followed as top contributors.

Geographical Distribution:

United Kingdom accounts for 87.63% of all transactions (3,280 orders).

Secondary markets include Germany (3.77%) and France (2.56%).

Weekly Sales Volume:

Monday is by far the highest-volume sales day with 1,559 transactions, significantly outperforming mid-week and weekend sales.

💡 Business Recommendations
Targeted Promotional Timing: Concentrate major promotional campaigns and inventory preparation leading into Thanksgiving Eve and early-week (Monday) launch windows.

Geographical Prioritization: Focus core marketing spend on the primary UK market while designing localized holiday campaigns to expand secondary European markets (Germany, France).
